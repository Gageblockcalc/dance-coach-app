Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. Applies to the Dance Coach website (dancecoach.pages.dev) and the Dance Coach Android app.

Summary

Dance Coach collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission only so it can track your body pose while you dance. All pose tracking runs on your device using a bundled model. Camera video, images and pose data are never uploaded, stored on our servers or shared with anyone.

Move to Earn (optional)

Move to Earn is off by default. If you turn it on and tap Start, the app reads your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) to count steps and shakes while the app is open, and uses the camera for squat and jumping-jack challenges. No location is collected. Motion readings are processed on the device and not stored. Only coin totals, outfit unlocks and daily counters are kept in local storage on your device, and the Reset button in Move to Earn erases them. Nothing is uploaded.

Microphone

The app does not record audio from your microphone.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, daily best scores, streak, level and badges, seasonal theme choice and finished seasonal events, recent scores, and per-move accuracy (a number per dance move, used for coach tips and practice routines; no images or video) are stored locally on your device. Unless you choose to sign in (below), nothing is uploaded and we cannot see them. Clearing app or browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

Optional sign-in to sync progress (website only)

You can play as a guest with no account. If you choose "Save my progress with a passkey", the website creates an account so your progress can follow you to other devices. We store only: a random account id; your passkey's public key (the private key and your fingerprint or face never leave your device); a session cookie (stored on our side only as a one-way hash, expires after 7 days); and a copy of your game progress (level, XP, badges, unlocks, daily and personal best scores, Move to Earn outfits you have unlocked, recent scores and per-move accuracy). Move to Earn coins, crates and daily counters are never synced; they stay on the device. No email, name, password, photos, video or camera data. Data is stored in Cloudflare D1. Sign out at any time; your progress stays on the device. Delete account (Your progress › Delete account) permanently erases the account, passkey record, sessions and synced progress from our servers right away; you can also remove the passkey from your device's password manager. To limit abuse, the server briefly counts requests per IP address and per account (counters expire within about 10 minutes). The Android app does not offer sign-in.

Clips

Video clips you record are created on your device and stay there unless you choose to download, save or share them yourself.

Notifications

Daily challenge reminders are optional, off by default, and scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Children

Dance Coach is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The optional sign-in stores no personal details, only the game progress described above.

Third parties

The Android app makes no network requests during normal use. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may process standard server logs (such as IP address) to deliver the site; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Links to other sites (for example Buy Me a Coffee) are governed by their own policies.

Helper chat (website only)

The website has an optional helper chat. It is off until you tap it. If you type or speak a question, the text is sent to our hub server (nick-sites-hub on Cloudflare) to get a short reply. The hub keeps only a daily request count for rate limits, not your messages. Push-to-talk uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may be processed by your browser provider (for example Google in Chrome); the microphone is used only while you hold the button. The Android app does not include the helper chat.

Sharing clips (website only, optional)

Sharing is off by default. A grown-up or user aged 13+ can turn on "Grown-up sharing" in Settings. A clip is uploaded only after you record it, tap "Share my dance" and confirm. Uploading needs a free passkey account on our hub (nick-sites-hub on Cloudflare); playing never does. Shared clips are public in the Community dances gallery. Every upload is checked by an automatic safety filter before it appears, and anything unclear waits for a human review. Location and device metadata (EXIF/GPS and similar tags) are stripped on the server. To report a clip, tap Report under it (3 reports hide it automatically). To delete your own clip, email tekjeep@gmail.com with your nickname and the clip, or remove it while signed in. The Android app does not include sharing.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Dance Coach is published by TekJeep. Questions or requests: email tekjeep@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/Gageblockcalc/dance-coach-app/issues.
