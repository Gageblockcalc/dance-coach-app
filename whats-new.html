What's new

1.0.x — October 2026

  • 📜 When a song ends, the page now scrolls to your results so you see your score right away on a phone.
  • 🔊 Count-in beeps are now timed by the music clock, so all of 3, 2, 1, go play on time even on a busy phone.
  • 📱 Screen stays on while you dance (on browsers that support it), so your phone won’t dim mid-song.
  • 🔊 Count-in beeps: hear 3, 2, 1 and a higher “go” beep on the first beat, so you can start on time without watching the screen.
  • ⬆️ Next grade goal: the results screen now tells you how much more you need for the next grade, like "4% more for grade B".
  • 🎯 Miss tips: when a beat is a miss, a short line under it tells you which arm or leg to fix, like "Fix your left upper arm".
  • 🅰️ Live grade: the score in the top corner while you dance now shows your grade too (like "72% · B"), and the "On fire" counter moved down so it no longer covers it.
  • 🔁 Dance again: one tap on the results screen starts the same song again and scrolls you back to the dance floor.
  • 🎉 Personal best cheer: beat your best score on a song and the results open with a big "New personal best!" banner, a fanfare and confetti. If you are close, it shows how far you are from your best so you can try again.
  • 📣 Challenge a friend: a big "Challenge a friend: beat my A · 85%" button now sits on the results screen, and your shared message includes your grade.
  • ⚡ Smoother on slower phones: in Auto quality, the glow around your body outline switches off when the phone starts to slow down, so dancing stays smooth. Colors and scoring stay the same.
  • 🔥 On fire: hit 10 or more beats in a row and the dance screen gets a glowing gold border with an "On fire" combo counter. With Reduce motion on, the border stays still.
  • 🎉 Better first seconds: your first good beat gets a "Nice start!" cheer with confetti, and misses in the first few beats no longer break your combo while you warm up.
  • 💪 Body-part report: after each song you see your strongest arm or leg and the one to focus on next time, with a match percentage for each.
  • 📷 Framing help: the countdown now tells you when you are fully in the frame, and if your feet or head get cut off, you are too close or off to the side, a short tip like "Step back so your feet show" pops up while you dance, so the camera can score every move.
  • 🟢 See exactly which limb to fix: while you dance, each arm and leg on your body outline lights up green when it matches the coach, yellow when it is close and red when it is off, with dots on every joint. Color-blind friendly colors use blue, white and orange instead.
  • 🥁 Music now flows like a real song: every 8 bars a drum fill and a rising whoosh build up, and a cymbal crash lands as the next part kicks in, so you can feel the big moments coming. Works with every song and the music volume slider.
  • 🏅 Every song now ends with a letter grade (S, A, B, C or D) next to your stars, and it shows on your result card too. Hitting a 10, 25, 50 or 100 beat combo now gets its own fanfare, a buzz and a big "🔥 combo!" callout.
  • ☁️ Play instantly as a guest, no sign-up. New, optional: save your progress with a passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock, no password or email) to carry your level, badges and unlocks to your other devices. Signing in on a device that already has progress combines the two, keeping the best of each. Sign out or delete your account any time from Your progress. (Website only for now.)
  • 🎉 Seasonal themes: Halloween, fall, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's, spring, summer and July 4th each bring a Seasonal stage, a coach outfit, a new tune and a seasonal event challenge (reach 60% for bonus XP and the Season star badge, once per season). Settings › Seasonal themes: Auto (by date), pick a season, or Off. Your result card now shows your level too.
  • 💡 Smart coach: live coach tips during a song (like "lift your left arm higher", can be turned off), a Song speed setting (0.8× to 1.2×), suggestions to speed up or slow down based on your recent runs (you always choose), and a 🎯 Practice routine built from your trickiest moves. The Daily challenge now grows with your level, with a goal score for bonus XP. All worked out on your device.
  • Accessibility: new Reduce flashing, High contrast and Color-blind friendly color settings. Hit words now always show a shape (★ Perfect, ◆ Great, ● Good, ✕ Miss), so color is never the only cue. On a keyboard, Space starts or pauses and P pauses. With Reduce motion on, only the important buzzes play.
  • New ⭐ Level system: earn XP from every song, the Daily challenge, streaks and badges (14 to collect). Level up to unlock 3 new songs (Candy Pop, Midnight Drive, Turbo Hype), 2 new stages (Aurora, Sunrise) and 3 new outfits. Everything you had before stays open. Your streak now forgives a missed day once in a while, the Daily challenge changes at your own midnight, and there is an invite-a-friend button.
  • Separate volume sliders for music, sound effects and the coach voice, each with its own on/off switch. New clean, family-friendly jokes and playful trash talk from Coach Disco Dan, with switches to turn either one off.
  • New, optional Move to Earn (off by default; turn it on in Settings): earn Move coins for steps, shakes and squats. Couch Potato slumps when you sit and dances when you move, squats make the rubber chicken lay golden eggs, and a mystery crate drops every 500 steps with silly coach outfits. Daily limits, no location, everything stays on your device.
  • Dance screen now fits your whole screen: camera on one side, a big coach on the other (desktop, TV, tablets and phones in landscape), with no scrolling mid-dance.
  • Menus in Spanish, German, Finnish, Russian, Arabic and Hebrew (right-to-left supported).
  • Better fit on phones with notches, punch-hole cameras and rounded corners.
  • Controls guide: tap or long-press ?, hold H, or hold View/Select on a controller.
  • Quality setting (Auto/Low/High) and sparkles on perfect moves.
  • Coins (web) and an email feedback link.
  • Android: targets Android 16.
