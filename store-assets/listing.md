# Dance Coach — Google Play listing (en-US)

**App name (≤30):** Dance Coach: Move & Score  
**Short description (≤80):** Dance with your camera, score your moves on-device, and keep a daily streak.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Dance Coach turns your phone into a friendly dance teacher. Prop your phone up, step back, and copy the coach's moves. The camera tracks your pose right on your device and scores how closely you match each move.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Daily challenge: a fresh 9-move routine every day. Keep your streak going.
• Free dance: pick a song and dance at your own pace.
• Real-time scoring: "Perfect!", "Great!" and "Good" feedback as you move.
• Coach Disco Dan: a family-friendly host with dad jokes and cheers. Optional voice, off by default.
• Record a clip of your dance to save or share yourself.
• Game controller support for menus, song select, start, pause and back.
• Works offline once installed. Original royalty-free music included.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Pose tracking runs entirely on your device. Camera video is never uploaded.
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• Reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Best with your whole body in view and decent lighting.
• English-first. Menus also come in Spanish, German, Finnish, Russian, Arabic and Hebrew (machine-quality translations, feedback welcome).
