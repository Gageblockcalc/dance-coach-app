Privacy

Short version: we collect nothing.

  • The camera is used only to track your pose, on this device. Video and pose data are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.
  • Optional Move to Earn (off by default): if you turn it on and tap Start, Dance Coach reads your phone's motion sensor to count steps and shakes, and uses the camera for squat challenges, only while the app is open. No location is collected. The coins and counters stay in local storage on this device and can be erased with the Reset button.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
  • Settings, best scores and streaks are stored only on this device (local storage). Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes them.
  • Clips you record stay on your device until you choose to save or share them.
  • Daily reminders are optional, off by default, and scheduled locally on your phone.
